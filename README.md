# Hip-Hop-Artist-Performance-Analysis

The Nigerian music industry has experienced significant growth in recent years, driven by the increasing global popularity of Afrobeats, Hip-Hop, and other contemporary African music genres. Digital platforms, particularly **YouTube**, have become important channels through which artists distribute their music, reach audiences, build fan communities, and measure engagement. As a result, data generated from digital platforms can provide valuable insights into how artists and their music are performing over time.

This analysis presents a **data-driven performance assessment of seven Nigerian music artists: Asake, Ayra Starr, Davido, Fireboy DML, Fola, Rema, and Wizkid**. The analysis uses key performance indicators such as **total views, total likes, total comments, subscribers, number of songs, and featured appearances** to examine the level of audience reach and engagement associated with each artist.

The dashboard provides an overview of the combined performance of the selected artists, with approximately **4.95 billion total views, 41.89 million likes, 2.18 million comments, and 22.29 million subscribers**, alongside **476 songs and 202 featured appearances** represented in the dataset. These indicators make it possible to examine not only the size of an artist's audience but also how actively that audience interacts with their content.

The analysis also examines performance from different perspectives. **Artist-level comparisons** are used to identify differences in views, likes, comments, subscribers, and collaborations. The **Top 10 Songs** sections highlight individual songs that generated high levels of views and likes, providing insight into the songs that attracted significant audience attention. In addition, the **yearly and monthly trend analyses** examine changes in views, likes, and comments over time, helping to reveal periods of increased or decreased audience activity.

The purpose of this analysis is not simply to determine which artist has the highest numbers, but to use data to understand **patterns in digital music performance and audience engagement**. By comparing multiple indicators, the analysis provides a broader picture of how music content performs across different artists, songs, and periods.

Overall, this project demonstrates how **data analytics and visualization can be applied to the entertainment and music industry**. By transforming raw performance data into interactive visual insights, stakeholders such as artists, managers, record labels, marketers, and content strategists can better understand audience behaviour, monitor performance, identify successful content, and make more informed decisions about future music promotion and digital strategies.


# Objectives of the Hip-Hop Artist Performance Analysis

## General Objective

The general objective of this analysis is to evaluate the digital performance and audience engagement of selected Nigerian Hip-Hop artists using data analytics and visualization, with particular focus on their YouTube performance.

## The analysis aims to:

Measure the overall performance of the selected artists using key indicators such as total views, likes, comments, and subscribers.
Compare the performance of individual artists to identify differences in their audience reach and engagement levels.
Analyze the number of songs released by each artist and examine how their volume of content relates to their overall digital performance.
Examine collaboration patterns by analyzing the number of features associated with each artist.
Identify the top-performing songs by views and determine which songs attracted the highest number of YouTube viewers.
Identify the top-performing songs by likes to understand which songs generated strong positive audience engagement.
Analyze yearly performance trends in views and likes to observe how audience engagement has changed over time.
Examine monthly trends in views, likes, and comments to identify periods of higher or lower audience activity.
Analyze audience interaction through comments, likes, and views to provide a broader understanding of engagement beyond simple view counts.
Demonstrate the application of data analytics and visualization in the Nigerian music industry by transforming raw music-performance data into meaningful insights.
Provide data-driven insights that can help artists, managers, marketers, and other music-industry stakeholders understand audience behaviour and digital content performance.

# Key Insights from the Hip-Hop Artist Performance Analysis

The analysis of the seven selected Nigerian artists reveals several important patterns in digital reach, audience engagement, content performance, and collaboration.

1. Rema recorded the highest total views

Rema recorded approximately 1.41 billion views, making him the artist with the highest total views among the artists analyzed. Ayra Starr followed with about 958 million views, while Asake recorded approximately 844 million views. This indicates that these artists generated substantial audience reach on YouTube.

2. Rema also recorded the highest number of likes

In terms of total likes, Rema led with approximately 12 million likes, followed by Asake with about 10 million and Ayra Starr with approximately 6 million. This shows strong audience interaction with the content of these artists.

3. Fireboy DML recorded the highest comments

Although Fireboy DML did not have the highest views or likes, he recorded approximately 1.03 million comments, the highest among the seven artists. This is an important insight because it shows that high views do not necessarily translate directly into the highest number of comments.

4. Rema had the highest subscriber count

Rema recorded approximately 565,000 subscribers, followed by Davido with about 513,000 and Wizkid with approximately 372,000. This suggests that Rema's YouTube channel had the largest subscriber base within the dataset.

5. Davido had the highest number of featured appearances

Davido recorded approximately 60 features, the highest among the artists analyzed. Fireboy DML followed with 35, while Wizkid had 31. This highlights the significant role of collaboration and featured appearances in the artists' music activities.

6. The dataset contains a large volume of music content

The analysis covers 476 songs and 202 features across the seven artists. This provides a substantial basis for comparing individual artists, songs, and audience engagement.

7. “Calm Down” was the highest-viewed song

The Top 10 Songs by Views analysis shows “Calm Down” at approximately 724 million views, followed by “Rush” at about 607 million views and “Essence” at approximately 372 million views. These songs contributed significantly to the overall view count in the dataset.

8. “Calm Down” also performed strongly in likes

“Calm Down” recorded approximately 5 million likes, placing it among the highest-liked songs in the dataset. “Lonely At The Top” also recorded about 5 million likes, while “Rush” recorded approximately 3 million likes.

9. Audience engagement changes across months

The monthly analysis shows considerable variation in views, likes, and comments. Some months recorded substantially higher activity than others, indicating that audience engagement is not constant throughout the year.

10. Yearly performance shows changing audience activity

The yearly analysis indicates that performance levels varied considerably across the years represented in the dataset. The highest annual view figure shown is approximately 1.58 billion, followed by lower figures in subsequent periods. This suggests that music performance can be influenced by factors such as song releases, viral trends, collaborations, and changes in audience behaviour.

11. Views alone do not explain overall performance

One of the major insights from the dashboard is that different artists lead different performance indicators. For example, Rema leads in views, likes, and subscribers, while Fireboy DML leads in comments and Davido leads in features. Therefore, evaluating an artist's digital performance using only one metric may provide an incomplete picture.

12. Digital analytics provides a broader understanding of music performance

The dashboard demonstrates how data analytics can transform music-performance data into actionable information. By examining views, likes, comments, subscribers, songs, features, and time-based trends together, it becomes possible to understand audience reach and engagement from multiple perspectives.

## Overall Key Finding

The analysis shows that digital music performance is multidimensional. While Rema recorded the highest views, likes, and subscribers in this dataset, other artists demonstrated stronger performance in specific areas such as comments and collaborations. The results therefore highlight the importance of using multiple performance indicators rather than relying on a single metric when analyzing an artist's digital presence.

# Eight data-driven Recommendations for the Hip-Hop Artist Performance

1. Focus on songs and content that generate high engagement

Artists should not focus only on getting views. They should study songs that generate both high views and high likes/comments and use those characteristics when planning future releases.

2. Use successful songs as a guide for future releases

The Top 10 Songs by Views and Likes show that some songs perform much better than others. Artists should analyze what made these songs successful—such as the topic, sound, collaboration, promotion, and release timing—and apply useful lessons to future songs.

3. Increase audience interaction

Since comments indicate active audience engagement, artists should encourage fans to comment, share, and participate through questions, challenges, behind-the-scenes content, and social-media discussions.

4. Strengthen collaborations

The dashboard records 202 total features, showing that collaborations are an important part of the artists' activities. Artists can collaborate with other popular or complementary artists to reach new audiences and potentially increase views, likes, and subscribers.

5. Pay attention to release timing

The monthly analysis shows differences in performance across months. Artists and their management teams should identify the months and periods with stronger engagement and use those insights when planning release and promotional campaigns.

6. Convert views into stronger engagement

Some artists have high views but comparatively lower likes or comments. This suggests an opportunity to improve viewer-to-fan conversion through stronger calls to action, engaging videos, storytelling, and content that encourages audiences to interact.

7. Learn from artists with strong engagement

The dashboard shows differences between artists in views, likes, comments, and subscribers. Each artist should identify the area where they perform strongly and the area where engagement can be improved, rather than relying on views alone.

8. Use data analytics continuously

Artists and their management teams should regularly monitor views, likes, comments, subscribers, song performance, and release periods. This will help them make data-driven decisions about music production, promotion, collaborations, and content strategy.
